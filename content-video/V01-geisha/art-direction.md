# Art direction v2: watercolor field notes of a seed

Thesis: the story is a seed travelling across a map and being rediscovered, so the frame is a painted
page. Every colour is a real watercolour pigment derived from the subject, and every scene owns one field.

Palette (pigments, not UI tints):
- Paper `#F3F2EE` (stone, chroma < 15%: not cream)
- Payne's grey `#26303A` (ink + dark field)
- Sap green `#2E5E3E` (Gesha forest field)
- Quinacridone crimson `#B0183F` (coffee cherry, the price field)
- Cerulean `#3B8FC2` (oceans: the journey)
- Hansa yellow `#F2C94C` (jasmine, active caption word)
- Leaf green `#8DB33A` (bergamot), blue-grey `#5E86A8` (Earl Grey), burnt sienna `#9C4A26` (rust spots only)

Field order, alternating lightness: paper → paper→sap → paper (cerulean globe) → Payne's grey → paper (glazes) → crimson → paper → sap.

Type: one family (Be Vietnam Pro), sentence case, five sizes far apart (230/150/92/60/42), tabular figures. No kickers, no italic-serif words, no pills.

Motion: one signature move — the wet bloom (a wash spreading from a point) opens each field. Second device used twice: the falling "I" (GEISHA → GESHA). No springs/overshoot; ease-out-expo.

Banned here (impeccable + taste-skill tells): near-black + neon, terracotta accents, tracked micro-labels, glows/drop shadows, bounce.
